Trail Buddy - Account Deletion Request

Trail Buddy users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by following the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email to the developer using the contact email shown on the Trail Buddy Google Play Store listing.
  2. Use the subject line: "Trail Buddy Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the email address associated with your Trail Buddy account so we can identify the account.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What Data Will Be Deleted

After your request is verified and processed, we will delete your Trail Buddy account and associated personal data that we control, where applicable. This may include account profile information and data associated with your account.

Data That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, for security, or to resolve disputes. Any retained data will be limited to what is necessary and kept only for the period required for that purpose.

Deletion Timeline

We will review verified requests and process them within 30 days, unless additional time is required by law or for legitimate technical or security reasons. If there is a delay, we will inform the requester.

Questions

For help with account deletion, contact the developer using the contact information on the Trail Buddy Google Play Store listing.
