TrailTayo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TrailTayo handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We May Collect

TrailTayo may access your device's location when you allow location permission, to help identify or display your current location and support location-related features.

If you use the AI Guide, the questions you submit may be sent to our backend service to generate responses. Technical information may also be processed to operate and troubleshoot the app.

TrailTayo uses Firebase Authentication if you use its sign-in features. Information processed depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide app features, display location-related information, answer AI Guide questions, maintain security, and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. Sharing of Information

Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate TrailTayo, such as authentication, hosting, AI, or map services, where those services are used. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Retention and Security

Information is retained only as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. Reasonable safeguards are used to protect information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Your Choices

You can manage location permission through your device settings. You may stop using the app at any time. Some features may not work if you deny required permissions.

6. Children's Privacy

TrailTayo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact the developer.

7. Third-Party Services

TrailTayo may rely on third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information provided on the TrailTayo Google Play Store listing.
